The problem

Foxelli runs seven DTC brands. Each one needed a steady stream of ad creative. The bottleneck wasn’t ideas. It was the gap between ‘we have an idea’ and ‘we have a finished video ad you can run on Meta tomorrow.’ That gap was eating my team’s week.

Architecture call I’d make again

The data layer and the media-generation layer are separated by design. Stateful business logic lives in Supabase where row-level security protects tenant data. Stateless heavy-lifting media calls run through n8n where queueing, retries, and provider fallback are trivial to wire. Splitting them by responsibility kept both layers honest.
