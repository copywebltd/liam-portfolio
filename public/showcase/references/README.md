Drop the reference ads you want to feature here (the AirPods "Silence is golden",
the Mastercard Venn, Colgate, the Kindle "uber driver" one, the Penn floppy-disk
billboard, etc). Any image format is fine.

into a small "studied from the greats" row inside the Creative section and redeploy.

just keep it a small, clearly-framed row, not a wall of other brands' work.
