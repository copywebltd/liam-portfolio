Strategy-First Generation

Every ad is grounded in a named advertising principle from a library of 99 (@The_AdProfessor) and cross-validated against David Ogilvy's 24 lessons + 38 rules. Claude Sonnet generates 3 concepts per batch, each with a different principle, clever idea, headline, image prompt, and complete Facebook ad copy.

Competitor Reverse Engineering

Upload a winning competitor ad and the system explains WHY it works (Ogilvy analysis across 10 dimensions), extracts its exact spatial layout as a JSON spec (headline position, product placement, stat badge zones), then rebuilds it for your brand while preserving the winning formula and swapping all content.

Intelligent Ad Matching

Curated library of winning ads tagged across 10 dimensions (~200 controlled vocabulary tags). Weighted Jaccard similarity scoring (campaign_goal 3x, industry 2.5x, tone 2x) matches brands to the best-performing templates. All running client-side with zero API calls.

Layout Zone System

Solved the problem of image AIs ignoring spatial instructions. Each layout zone's position and size gets converted to natural language ("across the top of the image, centered, a large prominent headline..."). Zones sorted top-to-bottom for natural reading order. Client-side canvas logo compositor for pixel-perfect brand placement.
